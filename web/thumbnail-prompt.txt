## ROBLOX R6 BODY

Every player character uses exact Roblox R6 proportions. This rule has no exceptions.

Head: Round with slightly flat chin. Slightly oversized for face readability — 26% of total body height.
Torso: Rigid rectangular block with soft rounded edges. Does not bend. 25% of height.
Arms: Rectangular segments with visible round ball-joint at shoulders. No wrist joints. 30% of height.
Legs: Rectangular segments with flat block feet. No ankle joints. 50% combined height.
Hands: Solid rounded blocks. No individual fingers.
Surface: Smooth gloss PBR plastic — like a Funko Pop rendered in Blender. No painterly texture. No realistic skin. No muscle definition. No human proportions. No R15 joint flexibility.

Monsters, bosses, and creatures use fully custom anatomy and are always significantly larger than the player character. They are not bound by R6 rules.

## THIN OUTLINE

Every player character has a thin dark continuous outline around their full outer silhouette.
Thickness: 1 to 1.5px. Color: #0A0A0A. No gaps. No breaks. Full continuous edge.
This is the Blender Solidify Modifier with Backface Culling look.
It makes characters pop from any background and stay readable at 200px.

## FACE — THE REASON PEOPLE CLICK

Every face shows extreme visible emotion. Neutral faces lose clicks. Every face must be readable at 200px wide.

Eyes: Large. White sclera clearly visible. Colored iris with dark outer ring. Dark contracted pupil. Defined eyelid lines top and bottom. Never the default Roblox minimal oval.
Eyebrows: Thick, dark, strongly shaped — the primary emotion signal at small sizes.
Mouth: Never neutral. Open with teeth for fear or shock. Gritted teeth for rage. Smirk for confidence. Full grin for excitement.
Face angle: Always 3/4 toward the viewer. Never pure side profile.
Face size: Fills 25-35% of the frame when the character is the main subject. Move camera closer if needed.

TERROR — highest CTR: Sclera fully visible around iris. Pupils contracted to tiny dots. Eyebrows at maximum height, inner corners pulling hard together. Jaw fully dropped open showing upper and lower teeth. Shoulders raised, body leaning hard backward, arms bracing or reaching out.

SHOCK: Eyes wide open, eyebrows raised symmetrically high. Mouth open in round O or showing upper teeth. Slight backward lean with hand near face or chest.

CONFIDENCE: Eyes half-lidded with direct gaze at viewer. One eyebrow slightly raised. Closed smirk or slight smile showing top teeth. Forward lean with item held firmly.

RAGE: Eyes narrowed. Eyebrows in deep V toward center. Gritted teeth with lips pulled back. Aggressive forward lean with fists or weapon raised.

EXCITEMENT: Eyes wide and bright with tiny specular shine dots in pupils. Eyebrows raised asymmetrically. Big open grin showing all teeth.

## POSE

Every character is mid-action unless the prompt says otherwise. No T-pose. No neutral flat standing.

Running: Arms pumping in opposite directions. Legs bent at different angles. Body leaning forward. One foot off ground.
Fear escape: Body twisting backward while moving. One arm reaching back toward the threat. Weight shifted reactively.
Holding or showing item: Arms raised or extended. Item clearly visible and readable. Body weight shifted toward it.
Attacking: Full weight shifted forward. Striking limb fully extended.
Being knocked: Body at diagonal. Limbs spread. Reactive expression.
Celebrating: Arms raised. Body upright and energetic.

## SIZE CONTRAST

Player character height in frame: 20-35% of total frame height.
Main threat, monster, giant object, or key item: 40-75% of total frame height.
This size gap creates instant drama and tells the story in a single glance. Apply to every image.

## COLORS

Use vivid bold saturated colors. Muted or washed-out palettes perform poorly.

The dominant background color should be strong and saturated — it sets the mood of the entire image. Bright game scenes use bold solid colors: orange, sky blue, neon green, bright teal, vivid purple. Dark game scenes use deep navy or near-black backgrounds but always include 1 to 2 emissive accent colors at full saturation to create a focal pop — glowing teal eyes, red energy, orange fire, white light beam. A dark image without a vivid accent reads as flat and dead.

The warm peach skin tone of the character (#F4C090 to #FFD4A8) must contrast clearly against whatever background color is used.

Highest CTR color pairs: red + blue, orange + teal, yellow + purple, neon green + black.

---

## LIGHTING

Every character has a visible rim light halo around their edges and hair from behind. This is always present. It separates the character from the background and makes the thin outline glow.

Dark scenes: Primary light is cool pale blue-white from behind and above. Secondary faint warm amber at the front reveals face detail only. Shadows are deep blue-purple — never pure black #000000.

Bright scenes: Bright front-top light. Everything well-lit and saturated. Short and soft mid-tone shadows.

The light source visible in the scene always matches the lighting direction on the characters.

---

## ENVIRONMENT

Every background is a real 3D-rendered environment with visible geometry and material detail. Never a painted backdrop. Never a blurry gradient.

Dark scenes: Tall trees with visible bark, wooden docks with plank gaps, concrete walls with cracks, water with ripples, mossy logs, rocky ground. Volumetric mist sits in the mid-ground between the characters and the background. Background geometry fades progressively with depth of field blur. Small pale floating particles are visible in the atmosphere. Sky or void visible at the top of the frame.

Bright scenes: Clean solid-color background or simple readable game geometry. Bright sky with cartoon clouds if outdoors. No fog. No blur. Everything sharp and vivid.

Ground plane always visible at the bottom of the frame with surface texture.

## COMPOSITION

Camera: Slightly low — looking up at subjects 10-15 degrees. Makes everything feel larger and more imposing.
Top 20% of frame: Reserved for sky or text. Never place character heads or key objects here.
Characters: Rule of thirds — lower center or lower left/right intersection.
Main threat or large object: Behind or beside the character, dominating the opposite side of the frame.

Depth layers:
1. Foreground ground plane with surface texture
2. Characters — sharp, outlined, expressive
3. Mid-ground — threat, large object, or key environmental element
4. Background — environment silhouettes fading into atmosphere, sky at top

Text (only when requested or present in reference): Large bold font in top 15-20% of frame. Rainbow gradient or bold single color (white, yellow, red) with thick dark drop shadow. Keep text short — 3 words maximum performs best.

## CHARACTER MATERIALS

Skin: Warm peach #F4C090. Lit areas #FFD4A8 with subsurface glow. Shadow areas warm brown with blue-purple tint. Slightly glossy — not matte, not cold.

Hair: Volumetric 3D geometry — individual cluster shapes, not a flat cap. Highlight stripe from primary light. Warm tones preferred: auburn #8B4513, red-orange #C0501A, warm brown #7B3F1A. Casts soft shadow on forehead.

Clothing: Always darker and less saturated than the skin so the face remains the focal point. Dark scenes: navy, black, charcoal. Bright scenes: medium-dark tones that contrast the vivid background. Visible soft fabric folds and slight specular on collar or buckle.
